# Keep patient data outside Nischit

**Status: accepted.** Nischit will not collect or store patient identities, patient test reports, diagnoses, or clinical histories. Usage events may count test-related reagent consumption without identifying a patient. Product Certificates of Analysis are allowed when they contain no patient data. This keeps the product focused on procurement/inventory evidence and reduces privacy, regulatory, and breach impact.
